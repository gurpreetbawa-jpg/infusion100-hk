Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and emergency contact details. On event day, we collect GPS location data while tracking is active. We may also import activity data from Strava if you choose to connect your account.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for organising the Infusion 100 charity walk event. This includes displaying participants on the live map, enabling the community feed, and allowing event organisers to contact you with important information.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Your first name and walk data may be visible to other participants through the community feed and live map. Your email, phone number, and emergency contact details are only visible to event administrators.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in our database. Strava tokens are encrypted at rest. Sessions are managed server-side with secure cookies.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact the event organisers or submit a data deletion request.

6. Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

7. Contact

For privacy-related queries, contact the Infusion 100 event organisers.
