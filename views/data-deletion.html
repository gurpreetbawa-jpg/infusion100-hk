Data Deletion Request

You can request deletion of all personal data associated with your Infusion 100 account. This includes:

  • Your profile information (name, email, phone, emergency contacts)
  • Walk and activity data
  • Location tracking history
  • Connected service tokens (e.g. Strava)

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. You will not receive a confirmation email — once processed, all data linked to your account will be permanently removed.
