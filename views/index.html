    About Infusion 100

    On the 12th December 2026, we are undertaking the Lantau Challenge to raise money for the 2027 Infusion100 School Build in Nepal.

    This year, we are walking in memory of our beloved friend and colleague, Gary Webster. Gary was a key sponsor of the first school walk and encouraged our “Chief” Linoy Kidd to go beyond the initial build and keep going.

    Since Infusion100 began in 2014, 3,000+ walkers have participated in 15+ walks in Guangzhou, Foshan, Shanghai, Hong Kong, London, Toronto, Mexico, Dubai and New York. Thanks to everyone's collective efforts, 10 schools have been built and over 350,000 USD has been raised.

    Sponsor our walk here: https://act.buildon.org/team/843985

    The Route

    From Discovery Bay in the east through Mui Wo, Pui O, Shek Pik, Ngong Ping, and Tai O, finishing at Tung Chung — 7 checkpoints across Lantau Island.
