    About Infusion 100

    Infusion 100 is a charity event bringing people together for a sponsored walk across the bridges of London. Choose your own start and end bridges, and walk any portion of the route that works for you.

    All funds raised go to the Infusion 100 charity.

    The Route

    From Hampton Court Bridge in the west to Tower Bridge, finishing at Canary Wharf — 26 bridges spanning London.
